At Yazoo Investment Srl, our heritage is deeply intertwined with the well-being of the Dominican people. Through our strategic partnership with the AFD-Barceló Foundation, we channel the success of our premium spirits into transformative community initiatives, ensuring that every drop of rum we produce contributes to a brighter future for the region.

award_star Values

Our foundation is built on integrity, transparency, and a profound respect for our origins. We believe that true luxury is not just in the craftsmanship of the spirit, but in the positive footprint we leave on the communities that nurture our production.

  • check_circle Empowering vulnerable families through sustainable support.
  • check_circle Preserving cultural heritage through education.

track_changes Mission

Our mission is to provide the neediest communities with the tools required for self-sufficiency. We prioritize children's education as the primary catalyst for change, ensuring that every child has access to quality learning environments and resources.

  • check_circle Building educational infrastructure in rural regions.
  • check_circle Facilitating yearly health and medical consultations.

What we have accomplished so far

school

1. Education Programs

Implementing curriculum-focused initiatives that teach values, environmental stewardship, and basic literacy to over 500 students annually.

elderly

2. Health Programs

Providing direct assistance and community support systems for the elderly, ensuring dignity and care for the region's seniors.

3. Medical Programs

Coordinating yearly mobile medical clinics that offer free consultations, vaccinations, and essential medicine to isolated communities.

Our commitment to the Dominican people is as deep as the aging process of our finest reserves. We will continue to grow, always ensuring our success is shared with those who make it possible.
