Production

100% Dominican Rum

At the heart of our operations lies the Alcoholes Finos Dominicanos (AFD) distillery, a beacon of modern production and ecological responsibility. As the largest distillery in the Dominican Republic, AFD sets the global standard for sustainability, achieving an impressive 98% green production rate. Our process integrates advanced thermal energy recovery and biomass utilization, ensuring that every drop of Yazoo rum respects the vibrant ecosystem from which it originates.

Raw Material

The Essence of Our Sugarcane

Superior rum begins in the fertile soils of the Dominican Republic. We exclusively source non-GMO sugarcane harvested at the peak of its sugar content. The proximity of our fields to the distillery ensures that the juice remains fresh, preserving the delicate floral and mineral notes that define the Yazoo profile. Our sustainable farming practices prioritize soil health and biodiversity, creating a legacy that tastes as pure as the land itself.

Technical Department

Scientific Precision, Masterful Craft

Our technical department is the bridge between ancient tradition and modern science. Led by a team of world-class master blenders and chemists, we monitor every stage of production through an ISO-certified laboratory. From yeast propagation to the final chromatographic analysis, our staff ensures that every bottle of Yazoo meets the most stringent international quality standards. It is this unwavering commitment to technical excellence that guarantees the consistent, award-winning flavor our clients expect.
