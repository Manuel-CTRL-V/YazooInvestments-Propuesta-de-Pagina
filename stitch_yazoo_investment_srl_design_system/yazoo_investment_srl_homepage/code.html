— Jose Enrique Yaber, Founder

Strategically headquartered in the heart of the Dominican Republic, Yazoo Investment Srl serves as a premier bridge between the Caribbean's rich spirit heritage and the international market. Our state-of-the-art aging facilities and master blenders collaborate with prestigious partners across five continents.

From bespoke small-batch aging to high-volume production, we maintain an uncompromising commitment to quality and traceability that has earned us a position of trust within the global spirits industry.
